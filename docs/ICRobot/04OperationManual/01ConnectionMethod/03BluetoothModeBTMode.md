## Operating Procedure

*Note: Make sure that Bluetooth is enabled on the computer.*

| <!-- 这是一张图片，ocr 内容为： -->
!\[](img/B5.png) | <!-- 这是一张图片，ocr 内容为： -->
!\[](img/B6.png) |
| --- | --- |
| **Step 1:** Turn on the robot. Press the Left button to switch between robot states. When `<SET>` is displayed on the screen, press the Center button to confirm and enter Settings mode. | **Step 2:** In the Settings menu, press the Left or Right button to select the connection mode. Select **BT** and press the Center button to confirm. The robot will then enter Bluetooth connection mode. |
| <!-- 这是一张图片，ocr 内容为： -->
!\[](img/B7.png) | <!-- 这是一张图片，ocr 内容为： -->
!\[](img/Z1.gif) |
| **Step 3:** No manual operation is required. The ICRobot will play one of the following voice prompts:<br/>+ **“Switching to Bluetooth mode.”** The robot will wait for the mode switch to complete.<br/>+ **“Bluetooth mode is already active. No switching is required.”** No waiting is required, and you can proceed directly to use the robot. | **Step 4:** Open the programming software on the computer, click **“Select Device”**, and select **ICRobot**. |
| <!-- 这是一张图片，ocr 内容为： -->
!\[](img/Z2.gif) | <!-- 这是一张图片，ocr 内容为： -->
!\[](img/Z03.png) |
| **Step 5:** Select the **Bluetooth** connection method and click **“Start Scanning”**. Select the device to connect to from the refreshed list of available Bluetooth devices.<br/>The Bluetooth device name consists of the **last four characters of the MAC address**.<br/> | \*\*\*\*After the connection is successful, the programming software will display the corresponding interface, and the robot will play the voice prompt **“Bluetooth connection successful.”** |



Note: If the computer does not have Bluetooth, you can purchase a Wi-Fi 6 wireless network adapter with built-in Bluetooth support and dual-band 5 GHz Wi-Fi transmission/reception capabilities. The model mentioned here is for reference only; you may also choose another compatible model according to your needs.
